# N-VA

A privacy-first platform for proving eligibility and reputation without exposing personal data.

## Why it matters
People often need to prove who they are or whether they qualify for access, funding, or opportunities. Traditional systems ask them to share sensitive information, creating privacy risk and friction.

## What N-VA does
N-VA lets users prove claims such as age, region, student status, reputation, and uniqueness while keeping the underlying data private.

## Core principle
Verify the claim, not the data.

## Problem statement
Existing verification flows require users to reveal too much information. That slows onboarding and undermines trust.

## Vision
Build a respectful identity layer where people can prove eligibility without leaking their personal details.

## User experience
A clean onboarding path for applicants and verifiers, with minimal disclosure and clear proof results.
